03 / System

One layer between your cluster and the market.

The promise is easy. The scheduling is not. Knowing which megawatts can move — which training jobs can wait, how much inference headroom exists this minute, what storage can absorb — and moving them without a single missed SLA is the entire problem. That is the engine we built.

L—01

Dispatch

Our scheduler models every workload against your service tiers — deferrable training shifts across hours, inference power shapes within seconds — and turns it into dispatch instructions. The fixed night-window schedule most operators still run now loses money outright in Germany and France: solar has inverted the price shape it was built on. Joule's scheduler re-plans every day at day-ahead publication, using only information that exists at decision time, and captures 84–90% of the theoretical optimum in all three launch markets. Fail-safe by design: when in doubt, your load follows its normal profile.

05 / The company

A software company. On the operator's side only.

Joule builds and operates the software layer between your compute and Europe's power markets. We are not an aggregator, not an energy retailer, not a hardware vendor, not a consultancy. We sell no electricity and resell no flexibility — you hold the market position and the grid relationship; our software works them for you.

We are not tied to any chip, cloud, or hardware stack. Your grid relationship is a strategic asset — it should not come bundled with someone else's silicon roadmap. Mixed fleets, sovereign AI factories, colocation operators: if your infrastructure answers to more than one vendor, you need a flexibility layer that answers to you.

The model is simple: the audit is free, the software is priced to be a rounding error, and we earn a share of the flexibility revenue we measure and settle. If you don't get paid, neither do we.
